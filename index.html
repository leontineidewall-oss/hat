06

The band

How a hat proves who it belongs to.

  1. The band has 30 cells. Light is 1, dark is 0.
  2. 24 of them come from your wallet address: the first 3 bytes of its SHA-256.
  3. 6 check cells make a misread fail instead of matching the wrong wallet.
  4. Each round reads every wearer's X picture once, finds the band and compares it to their wallet.

08

Questions

Do I have to give up my pfp?

No. That's the point of the hat: your own picture stays, the hat sits on top.

Where does the money come from?

The creator fees from every $HAT trade go to the fee wallet. Each round splits that wallet between the wearers, by holdings.

What if I take the hat off?

You're not counted in the rounds while it's off. Put it back on and check again.

Can someone steal my hat?

They can wear it, but the band carries your wallet's code. Every round it counts for your wallet, not theirs.

Do I connect a wallet?

Never. You paste the address you want paid. Nothing here asks you to sign anything.
